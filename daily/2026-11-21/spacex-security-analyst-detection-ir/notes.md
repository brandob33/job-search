SpaceX — Security Analyst (Detection and Incident Response) (Level I/II), Hawthorne, CA. Onsite.
Pay: Level I $95,000-$115,000/yr; Level II $110,000-$130,000/yr.
RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Serve as frontline responder in the SpaceX Security Operations Center (SOC), identifying, triaging, and remediating security detections and anomalies.
- Support incident response by investigating incidents from initial detection through root cause.
- Help collect evidence and work with engineering teams to harden systems against future attacks.
- Contribute to detections that target sophisticated attacker tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs).
- Improve endpoint and network visibility and detection and response playbooks.
BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in cybersecurity, computer science, engineering, information technology, or another STEM discipline, or 2+ years of professional information security experience in lieu of a degree.
- Experience with operating system auditing on Linux, Windows, or macOS.
- Experience with network and host-based collection tools.
PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:
- Knowledge of classic and emerging threat actor TTPs across pre- and post-exploitation.
- Understanding of incident response processes.
- Experience with Elastic, Splunk, and/or other SIEMs.
- Scripting for automating security operations and incident response.
- Reverse engineering, C2 exploitation, and system and network forensics.
- Cloud infrastructure and cloud security knowledge.
- Community contributions (blog posts, conference talks, CTFs, tool development).
- Certifications such as SANS/GIAC, OSCP, or similar.
ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Onsite in Hawthorne, CA (no remote/hybrid). Extended hours and weekends as needed. Occasional travel.
- ITAR: U.S. citizen/national, lawful permanent resident, refugee, or asylee.
