- Reverse engineering, C2 analysis, and forensics.
- Cloud security knowledge.
- Community contributions; certifications such as GIAC or OSCP.
ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Onsite in Hawthorne (no remote/hybrid). Extended hours and weekends as needed. Occasional travel.
- ITAR: U.S. citizen, lawful permanent resident, refugee, or asylee.
