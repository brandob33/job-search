- Strong written and verbal communication, analytical, and organizational skills.
- Ability to manage multiple assignments at once.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.
- Ability to handle sensitive information with discretion.

Preferred
- Interest in cybersecurity, risk, compliance, or IT; familiarity with security terminology and concepts.
- Coursework or certifications such as ISC2 CC, CompTIA Security+, Google Cybersecurity Certificate, or Microsoft SC-900.
