## Posting (excerpt)
Millennium Space Systems (A Boeing Company) — Cybersecurity Analyst (Level 1-2), El Segundo, CA. Onsite, special-access facility.
Pay: Level 1 $35.96-$52.88/hr; Level 2 $42.91-$63.10/hr.
RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Run daily and weekly audits and scans with SIEM, vulnerability scanners, antivirus software, IDS/IPS, Nmap, Wireshark, and similar tools.
- Create and maintain Risk Assessment Reviews, Disaster Recovery plans, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- Assess and implement the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF), Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), and NIST 800-171 on the corporate network.
- Monitor network activity with IT, produce weekly reports, and brief the head of Information Security.
- Create, modify, and upgrade SIEM alerts and dashboards.
- Create, modify, and upgrade vulnerability management scans and reports.
- Serve as liaison for corporate Information Security matters.
- Plan or lead risk management activities: disaster recovery, continuity of operations, incident response.
- Administratively support security policy development and maintenance.
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 8570/8140 IAM certification: Security+ or equivalent.
- 1-2 years of SOC, Information Security Analyst, or equivalent academic experience.
- Strong work ethic and ability to learn complex SOC concepts.
- Experience in Linux and Windows integrated environments.
- Incident response experience or related academic knowledge.
- Familiarity with SIEM, NIDS/NIPS, vulnerability scanning, centrally managed endpoint protection, and network threat hunting.
- Working knowledge of NIST 800-171 or related cybersecurity frameworks.
- Strong writing skills; explain technical concepts to non-technical people.
- Applicants MUST be U.S. citizens and eligible for a security clearance; willing to apply for and maintain a clearance.
PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
- 8570/8140 IAM certifications Security+ or higher.
- A.S. or B.S. in computer science, computer engineering, cyber security, or related.
- Familiarity with SIEMs, IDS/IPS, vulnerability scanners, DLP, antivirus.
- Digital forensics experience. Familiarity with CMMC. Familiarity with cybersecurity investigations.
