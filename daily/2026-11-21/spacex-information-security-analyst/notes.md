SpaceX — Information Security Analyst (Level I/II), Hawthorne, CA. Onsite.
Pay: Level I $95,000-$115,000/yr; Level II $110,000-$130,000/yr.
RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Manage and triage the Information Security ticket queue and provide technical support to employees and stakeholders.
- Perform operational tasks to keep security platforms and services reliable and available.
- Develop scripts and lightweight automation for support and operational processes.
- Document procedures, run books, and troubleshooting guides.
- Work with Security Engineers on investigations, improvements, and larger security projects.
- Support and maintain YubiKeys, SSH infrastructure, certificate management, and access control systems.
- Analyze software before installation on SpaceX systems for signs of malicious behavior.
BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in cyber security, computer science, engineering, information technology, or another STEM discipline, or 2+ years of professional information security experience in lieu of a degree.
- Experience with operating system internals on Linux, Windows, or macOS.
- Scripting experience in Bash, Python, PowerShell, or other languages.
PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:
- Managing a ticket queue and providing strong customer service in a fast-paced technical environment.
- Linux systems administration and troubleshooting.
- Scripting (Bash, Python, or similar) and software engineering practices.
- Understanding of security principles (authentication, access control, encryption) and malware/software analysis.
- Automating repetitive support tasks and building internal tools.
- Familiarity with enterprise security tools.
- Hands-on experience with YubiKeys, SSH, public key infrastructure (PKI), and basic security concepts.
- Explaining technical concepts to technical and non-technical users.
- Problem-solving mindset and drive for continuous improvement; comfort in a fast-paced environment.
ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Onsite only (no remote/hybrid). Extended hours and weekends as needed. Travel up to 10%.
- ITAR: U.S. citizen/national, lawful permanent resident, refugee, or asylee.
